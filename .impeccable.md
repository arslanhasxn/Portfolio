## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to Arslan Hasan’s personal portfolio (recruiters, collaborators, curious peers). Context is exploratory and brief — they should understand who he is and leave with a memorable interaction.

### Brand Personality
Dry, playful, precise. Voice is monospace-minimal with one witty object (the business card). No corporate polish, no neon excess.

### Aesthetic Direction
Brutalist black canvas, Geist Mono UI chrome, mobile composition locked and centered on all viewports (empty side gutters on desktop). High contrast gray-on-black text; personality concentrated in the card (condensed name, lime character, dithered portrait). Anti-references: purple gradients, heavy glows, soft UI chrome, dashboard clutter.

### Design Principles
1. One composition — the page is a single sparse frame, not a scrolling brochure.
2. Chrome stays quiet; the card is the only expressive surface.
3. Motion is purposeful: L→R text wave at rest, spring focus + drag, clean 3D flip — never decorative glow.
4. Mobile scale is the source of truth; desktop only adds empty space.
5. Assets carry the brand — card faces, arrow, and favicon are design artifacts, not placeholders.
